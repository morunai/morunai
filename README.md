Software engineer by profession. Overengineering by choice.

I write backend code, look after AWS infrastructure, and lately spend a fair amount of time giving AI agents tools and then watching closely what they do with them.

Most of my projects start as "this should take an afternoon." Some of them are even finished.

## Things I do

- **Backend engineering**: services, APIs, and the plumbing in between.
- **Cloud infrastructure on AWS**: building it, running it, and reading the bill afterwards.
- **Developer tooling & automation**: spending three hours automating a ten-minute task, on principle.
- **AI experiments & agentic workflows**: LLMs, tool use, and the space between "it works" and "it works twice".
- **Occasional questionable side projects**: built mostly because nobody stopped me.
